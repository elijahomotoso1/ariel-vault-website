03 / Selected consulting experience

Enterprise experience.
Applied with perspective.

The consultancy draws on more than 20 years of industry experience. These selected engagements illustrate how governance requirements and technical delivery come together.

Public sector01 / NATIONAL IDENTITY

Bringing privacy and security into a national identity programme.

Turning newly introduced data protection requirements into practical programme and design requirements.

The challenge

A national identification programme in Jamaica needed to address new data protection legislation alongside the security and privacy implications of protecting citizens’ identities.

The engagement

The engagement focused on reviewing the legislation, identifying gaps in earlier requirements and working with design and implementation teams to revisit concerns around privacy, identity protection and data sovereignty. Technology partners were engaged to assess how proposed solutions supported programme objectives.

Contribution & outcome

Previously overlooked concerns were brought back into the requirements process. Requirements were revised and identified gaps addressed with the delivery teams.

Pharmaceuticals02 / GLOBAL TRANSFORMATION

Enabling secure collaboration through a complex merger.

Supporting the foundations of a new technology environment as teams transitioned across organisational boundaries.

The challenge

A global pharmaceutical programme spanning 17 sites was establishing a new cloud-based environment. Users needed to communicate across three companies while migration and organisational transition were under way.

The engagement

The engagement focused on departmental data retention requirements, email services, user identities and federation. Technical work supported secure email flow between the participating companies while users continued working across existing and new environments.

Contribution & outcome

The cross-company email and federation challenge was resolved during the engagement, supporting secure communication through the transition. Data retention policies were developed around departmental requirements.
